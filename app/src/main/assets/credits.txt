SM64 Handheld is an Android handheld fork built on these projects:

Android port by VDavid003
https://github.com/VDavid003/sm64-port-android-base

sm64ex engine and enhancements
https://github.com/sm64pc/sm64ex
https://github.com/VDavid003/sm64-port-android

Render96 model pack by the Render96 team (optional download)
https://github.com/Render96/ModelPack

Render96 HD Texture Pack by PokeHeadroom and its contributors (optional download; GPL-3.0 project)
https://github.com/pokeheadroom/RENDER96-HD-TEXTURE-PACK

SDL2 by Sam Lantinga and contributors (zlib license)
https://wiki.libsdl.org/SDL2/FAQLicensing

Apache Commons Compress 1.19 (Apache License 2.0)
https://commons.apache.org/proper/commons-compress/

XZ for Java 1.9 by the Tukaani project (public domain)
https://github.com/tukaani-project/xz-java

Super Mario 64 is Nintendo's game. You must supply your own original US ROM. Model and texture packs are downloaded separately.
